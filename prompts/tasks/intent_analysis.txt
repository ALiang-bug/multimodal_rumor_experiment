Infer the primary communicative purpose supported by an observable cue and its context. PUBLIC_INTEREST requires an identifiable beneficiary and public information, safety or assistance appeal. ECONOMIC_INTEREST requires evidence of sales, monetization, fundraising or another economic return. POLITICAL_INTEREST requires an expressed political position, beneficiary or call to political action. PSYCHOLOGICAL_FULFILLMENT requires cues of entertainment, attention, self-expression or validation. Topic alone cannot establish purpose. If several purposes remain equally supported or no cue meets a threshold, use UNKNOWN alone and identify the uncertainty.
